  external prerequisites honestly, never mark an unavailable feature complete.

  This necessary cross-repository companion is not a third product part.
- Preserve existing CRM working-tree changes. Build from an isolated clean
  checkout when modifying its code; never overwrite unrelated local work.

  plan. Producer flag OFF; no production mutation/deployment or historical push.

  assumed. Secrets remain in existing secret stores, never repository contents.
- Owner/accountant decisions still needed for inventory,
  mixed-use expenses, tax/tip treatment and annual form version.
