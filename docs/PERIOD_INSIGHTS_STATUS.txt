Account period insights — 2026-10-20

Integrated block: authenticated account overview; month/quarter/calendar-year
presets and custom inclusive UTC event-date periods (up to 366 days); comparison
with the preceding equal-length period; zero-filled monthly breakdown; full-ledger
aggregation; drill-down to source journal lines and transaction provenance;
unresolved-event and missing-payment-date visibility; EN/RU; access regression.

The database computes current, previous, months and attention in one SQL statement.
Summaries use all authorized postings, not a client page. PostgreSQL numeric sums
and decimal strings retain precision; client BigInt computes display metrics.
Client validates balances, period identity, complete month coverage and exact
monthly reconciliation to period amounts/counts before rendering anything.

Metrics: recorded receipts, customer refunds, net receipts, parts cost net of
supplier credits/corrections, fees net of fee refunds, their recorded difference,
clearing movement and payable movement. Accrued parts remain visible separately
through payables and missing payment dates. Neither movement is a closing balance.
These are event-date operational facts, not tax recognition or cash-basis reports.
Unrecorded operating expenses and owner transfers are not invented. Bank/receipt
reconciliation and approved tax mapping are explicitly unavailable. No finalized,
approved or tax-ready status is exposed, even for an empty account.

Direct code rows drill into the same period/code; balanced transactions retain all
lines. Derived figures are explained by formula and can be traced via all-period
transactions. Month and prior-period drill-downs have explicit date scopes. Return
refreshes the same overview period. Account changes reset state and abort requests.
