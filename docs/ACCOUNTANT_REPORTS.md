# Accountant working papers

Current workflow: see [Statement handoff and parts register](STATEMENT_HANDOFF_AND_PARTS.md). The owner categorizes cash income/expenses and hands the saved Statement to the accountant. No accountant approval is required in the application. New snapshots use `Prepared for handoff`; historical v1 snapshots retain `Accountant Review` solely as their original recorded status. The migration 0020 and rollback notes below describe the original release only; for databases with 0021–0023, follow the compatibility and recovery instructions in the current workflow document.

Reports are scoped to one authorized business account of Aksenov LLC. The report is not a consolidated company return. Personal/family workspaces cannot read, save or map this form. Only an explicitly shared, paid personal/family expense can contribute its chosen business share. USD is currently the only ledger currency; cross-currency attribution is rejected.

The report uses UTC actual-payment dates and integer cents, with all 44 reference-form rows including zeros. Original operational classifications remain unchanged. Form allocations are versioned and must sum exactly to the eligible line; clearing an allocation appends a revision. No automatic tax deduction, inventory policy or mixed-use percentage is inferred. Unmapped activity and reconciliation gaps remain visible. Saving prepares a handoff snapshot, not a tax filing or accountant approval.

Personally paid purchases do not create a second journal transaction. Linking an existing business reimbursement excludes that payment and its subsequent refunds from another report expense. Returned reimbursements reduce the all-date reimbursement balance. Refunds of the original personal purchase require review of the business share. Cancel reimbursements before canceling a purchase link; cancellation is append-only and permits a corrected link. A new link does not inherit the old category proposal. Saved snapshots remain unchanged.

Saving checks the SHA-256 of the full preview inputs and fails on stale data. Command IDs support exact retry after a lost response. Source changes after snapshot creation belong to a new snapshot. Saved report data includes source journals, original operational allocations, proposed revisions and evidence hashes. PDF/CSV exports require a saved snapshot; browser PDF uses its own print document, native PDF uses Expo Print. Exports are preliminary working papers, not signed financial statements.

Limits are explicit: at most 366 days, 5,000 eligible lines, 100 saved snapshots and 1,000 historical personal links per account. Large reports fail rather than truncate totals. Account-level reports must not be added together without reviewing shared purchases and reimbursements. All-date reimbursement balances are clearly separate from report-period totals.

Migration 0020 adds tables/functions only. Apply after a staging backup and verified migration hashes 0001–0019. Roll back application code by redeploying the preceding tested commit; retain the additive audit tables. For database recovery restore the snapshot into a separate branch, verify hashes/permissions and recover only the intended Nestwise database. Do not rewind the shared Neon branch in place, because it also hosts the isolated CRM test database. CI dumps/restores the disposable PostgreSQL database and checks table fingerprints, immutable history and restricted permissions.

Verification includes real auth/ACL tests, personal/family rejection, split proposals, stale writes, frozen replay, personal-source permission checks, duplicate prevention, reimbursement refunds, append-only cancellation/re-linking, formula/HTML escaping, exact large cents, PostgreSQL concurrent proposal/snapshot saves and restored snapshot protection. Runtime web/native printing still needs platform acceptance; a local PDF conversion of the same HTML is layout evidence, not proof of Expo native printing.
