# Statement handoff and parts register

The owner prepares categorized cash income/expenses for Aksenov LLC and hands the Statement to the accountant. Accountant approval, inventory-tax policy approval and in-app tax finalization are not prerequisites. Categories do not calculate tax deductions. Personal/family workspaces cannot access the business form; only explicitly selected business portions of existing personal purchases are included.

New saved reports use statement-handoff-v2 / Prepared for handoff. V1 reports remain readable, retain their input hashes and historical status, and cannot be edited. All 44 original categories remain available. Missing categories/dates, unreconciled transactions, source problems and unknown stock payment dates remain visible. Save a new snapshot after changing categories or stock evidence; stale previews fail closed.

Parts are separate from cash posting. A lot records quantity, exact unit purchase cost, optional actual payment date, note and an optional existing journal payment. Blank date means unknown, never today's usage date. Consumption and return store job reference/date/quantity and append audit revisions. Returns cannot exceed that job's net consumption. Competing commands use optimistic revisions and database serialization; retries use the same command ID. Only an unused lot can be archived. An archived lot retains evidence and its payment allocation to avoid reusing the same purchase for duplicate stock. Correct a payment allocation by updating its evidence before archiving.

One payment may fund multiple lots; the sum of linked lot purchase costs cannot exceed its unrefunded amount. Personal payments must first have an explicit business-share link to the selected business account. The register does not move funds, create cash expenses, automatically import historical purchases or push stock consumption into CRM. Costs known only from memory stay evidence, outside cash totals, until the owner identifies/posts the actual original payment. Later supplier refunds require reviewing existing allocations; the register is not a supplier inventory accounting system.

Purchase search uses accessible existing payments, merchant/note substring, optional UTC date limits and minimum amount. Blank dates search all history. More than 100 results fails with a request to narrow the search; nothing is silently truncated. Bank-origin payments show the source document hash and bank date. Unbooked bank CSV rows can be searched by date, description or amount under Bank, then explicitly linked/booked through the existing duplicate-protected workflow. Equal amounts do not automatically establish a match. Arbitrary scanned bank PDFs still require a supported CSV conversion or representative bank samples; no unverified bank PDF parser is claimed.

Migrations 0021–0023 add stock history, handoff report versioning and purchase search. Apply only after a verified staging snapshot and checks of all prior migration hashes. Never edit applied migrations. Recovery: preserve additive history; restore to a separate Neon branch/database, not in-place on the shared CRM branch. The previous app's live-report parser understands only v1, so app rollback requires a compatible report_input wrapper or a separately restored staging database; do not blindly redeploy PR20 against the v2 report API. Saved v1/v2 payloads must be retained. No production/schema rollback or historical data deletion is authorized.

Android installation and physical-device acceptance are deferred while the owner is away with the disconnected phone. The startup fix is in PR20 / 1.0.2; this block does not publish Android/iOS or production. No Apple Developer account exists. The CRM staging canary remains disabled.
