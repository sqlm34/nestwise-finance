Test identity lifecycle
scripts/auth-staging-proof.cjs provisions a random proof-UUID@nestwise.test user
with a random password and an empty synthetic workspace/account. No customer
records, transactions or email messages are involved. The proof identity is
retired after browser verification: its credential password becomes NULL and all
of its sessions are removed. Its synthetic workspace remains as audit evidence.
This is not the user's real account or an invitation mechanism.
